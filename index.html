The journey

Over 30 years in the plumbing of the internet.

Same builder brain, different era. I started when web pages were hand-typed and dial-up screamed down the phone line. I looked after the systems people never saw, and I helped people who were stuck. That habit has stayed with me.

  1. Mid ’90s
    PowerUp BBS
    My first steps online, on a 2400-bps modem.
  2. 1996
    First web pages
    Hand-built HTML, graphics and desktop publishing.
  3. 1997–2001
    StarVision Access
    Remote ISP support, customer service and internet operations.
  4. Dec 1997
    JohnFarnham.com
    I registered the domain and started the fan site with Fiona.
  5. 1998–2002
    Gotham Technologies
    Managing Director. Websites, computers, networks and business technology.
  6. 2008 →
    Fans Forever
    I created John Farnham Fans Forever on 2 June 2008 and still look after it.
  7. 2026
    Building again
    Designing and testing apps, websites and practical tools, with AI in the toolkit.
